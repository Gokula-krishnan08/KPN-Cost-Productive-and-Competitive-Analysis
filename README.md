# Kovai Pazhamudir Nilayam (KPN) Snacks Market Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive business analysis of Kovai Pazhamudir Nilayam (KPN) snack products using Power BI. The dashboard evaluates production costs, operational expenses, supply chain efficiency, pricing strategies, competitor positioning, and customer preferences to generate actionable insights for business growth and profitability.

The analysis focuses on the following snack products:

- Butter Murukku
- Ragi Murukku
- Kaara Boondhi
- Ribbon Murukku
- Tapioca Chips

The primary objective of this project is to identify cost optimization opportunities, assess market competitiveness, understand consumer behavior, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze production and operational costs across snack products.
- Evaluate supply chain efficiency and identify improvement opportunities.
- Compare competitor pricing and packaging strategies.
- Understand customer preferences and purchasing behavior.
- Assess product profitability and market positioning.
- Generate actionable recommendations to improve revenue and operational performance.
